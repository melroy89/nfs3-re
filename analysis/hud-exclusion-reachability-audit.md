# HUD exclusion reachability and worklist capacity

## Result

The 28-fragment witness in `hud-fragment-worklist-capacity-model.json` uses four arbitrary rectangles and is not a default-layout witness. In a fresh per-view geometry build, the installed default selector values enable at most two geometry exclusion producers; the separate conditional producer can add at most one record, but it is restricted to the police-role view while the fourth geometry site is restricted to the non-police role. The two cases cannot combine to make a third default record:

- Police-role view: geometry site 4 is gated off; default sites 2 and 3 are gated off; site 1 and the conditional producer may contribute one each.
- Non-police view: the conditional producer is gated off; default sites 2 and 3 are gated off; sites 1 and 4 may contribute one each.

Thus the default path contributes at most two rectangles to a given per-view list. The exhaustive caller model found a maximum of **11 worklist fragments** for every two-rectangle case, including pairwise-disjoint cases, below the 15-record local arrays. This establishes a default-layout capacity bound under the reviewed ordinary finite rectangle/helper contracts. It does not establish the same bound for user-modified selector combinations or stale state across unreviewed layout transitions.

For ordinary finite HUD edits, `00459db0` converts descriptor pixel coordinates and inclusive right/bottom endpoints into normalized layout values, then calls `00459670`. The checker rejects components below zero or above one and applies the split-session top/bottom half rule, with an ID-8 exception; its direct comparisons do not establish a general NaN policy, and it does not enforce endpoint ordering. Its dossier is `ev_fcac754f490bc22653dc1071aef367102f9d8ea9a536c206b260ebfd5c9dc929`, with caller normalization dossier `ev_e6dd3c727744559e7a38bfb2944910f42d538413c12c775c6e9819cc95c43e87`. Placement-conflict checks further restrict ordinary accepted edits. The four producer inputs are therefore tied to specific layout items (slots 9, 3, 1, and 16), plus the role/profile gates above.

Default initialization sets every `006fbc6c` field to 1, so site 2 is disabled; it sets every `006fbc68` field to 3, so site 3 is disabled. `006fbc5c` is initialized from `(driverFlags & 6)==0`, so site 1 may or may not run; site 4 may run only for a non-police view in race mode 3. The conditional producer is police-only. This yields the two-record default upper bound independent of the actual viewport dimensions or endpoint positions.

## Nondefault selector co-reachability and count lifecycle

The HUD layout editor has concrete paths to the selector values that activate sites 2 and 3. In the editor update block, six row/role cells are traversed (row index `EDX=0..2`, role variant `ECX=0..1`); separate shadow fields feed `006fbc6c` and `006fbc68` for each cell, with no test that makes one selector's value conditional on the other:

- `006fbc6c` is loaded at `0045edf9`; nonzero values map to shadow `value-1` at `0045ee03/06`, while zero maps to shadow 3 at `0045ee0e`. The update path reads that shadow at `0045f1f8`, wraps shadow 3 to zero at `0045f201/20e`, and otherwise stores shadow+1 at `0045f203/206`. The editor state therefore represents selector values 0–3; the producer's active values 2 and 3 are reachable in its own cycle.
- `006fbc68` is loaded at `0045ef21`; nonzero values map to shadow `value-1` at `0045ef31/34`, while zero maps to shadow 4 at `0045edd1`. The update path reads that shadow at `0045f2bc`, wraps shadow 4 to zero at `0045f2c8/cb` through `0045f1d2/d4`, and otherwise stores shadow+1 at `0045f2d1/d4`. The editor state represents selector values 0–5; the producer's active values 2 and 4 are reachable in its cycle.
- The commit loop starts by clearing the current `006fbc68` cell at `0045f1d2/d4`, then processes all row/role cells through the independent shadow fields. The row loop increments `ECX` to 2 at `0045ee92/96`; the role loop increments `EDX` to 3 at `0045ef3f/40`, yielding six cells. Thus the code has no mutual-exclusion gate between site 2 and site 3 for one active row/role bank. Instruction evidence includes the shadow read `ev_ca972ff802c14534d6f319e03f3efa23752930f75a446884db19dfd4c946283d` (`0045f2bc`), selector writes `ev_4e51fd1f48b14d1b9ce580bc836eaf622813661e254b37c27b0a3fc6a980c21b` (`0045f206`) and `ev_7b906bb820419e5485791ce0d4b383e8183e3034a7e5641acc12cd47a28023e0` (`0045f2d4`), plus the wrap/reset instructions `ev_aaadf724892d073fa664868ea9e652434bec417632783cce668a996b2e3cb3be0` (`0045f1d4`), `ev_9162345d7f833cd8350afbbc3c29ff75d3c472a14ce7053238f91ffe38d4e30e0` (`0045f28e`), and `ev_0ec4679f996a3629e2c6a08c13f2fbe0486badbeb84142d1935a0a058214c1a70` (`0045f2df`).

Consequently, at the producer gate level, a non-police view in race mode 3 can enable sites 2, 3, and 4 together, with site 1 additionally enabled when its view-zero, `006fbc5c`, and `00422aa0` gates are true. A police view can enable sites 1–3 and the conditional producer; site 4 is false for that role. This establishes possible selector/role co-occurrence in the code, not that four slot-derived rectangles realize the existing synthetic coordinates. The 0–1 endpoint bounds and placement-conflict checks in `00459db0/00459670` apply to edited descriptor coordinates, while per-view inset/rounding and profile admission still constrain the transformed rectangles.

`UpdateHudLayoutGeometryForView` clears the per-view counter before its four sequential producer sites. `RenderRaceHUD` may subsequently run the conditional update before calling the map renderer. Under a stable role/geometry bank, the role split limits the combined source list to at most four records. The conditional updater appends only when its state changes to active and decrements the list count when it changes to inactive, so repeated render calls do not accumulate duplicate conditional rectangles. A role transition with an old geometry list still resident could leave a non-police site-4 record while the police-only conditional record is appended; whether the transition always triggers a geometry clear first is not established. There are exactly five static call references to `00487350`—the four geometry sites plus this one conditional site—so the bounded producer inventory is at most five distinct current records, absent counter corruption. No unbounded producer loop or persistent append accumulation was found.

## Exhaustive two-exclusion caller model

The retained script [model_hud_bounded_exclusion_capacity.py](scripts/model_hud_bounded_exclusion_capacity.py) reuses all 16 original-helper templates from [rectangle-fragment-mask-audit.json](evidence/probes/rectangle-fragment-mask-audit.json) and the reviewed replace/append/delete/restart logic in [model_hud_fragment_worklist_capacity.py](scripts/model_hud_fragment_worklist_capacity.py). The reproducible result is [hud-bounded-exclusion-capacity.json](evidence/probes/hud-bounded-exclusion-capacity.json).

For each axis, the model enumerates all ordered pairs of endpoints over six representative levels, including ties and zero-width intervals, against one source interval. Four exclusion endpoints plus the two source edges require no more than six ordered levels; positions outside the source clip to the nearest source edge before the helper selects its mask and output template. The Cartesian product covers 194,481 two-rectangle cases, of which 170,456 are pairwise disjoint. The model records both the maximum transient worklist count across every mutation in every case and the final list count; each maximum is **11** in both the unrestricted and pairwise-disjoint subsets. It separately aggregates `first_overflow` over all cases, not just the maximum witnesses: no case transiently appends to record 15 or later. One maximum witness is `[1,2,2,3]` and `[3,1,4,4]` against source `[0,0,5,5]` (all coordinates can be affinely rescaled to a viewport rectangle).

## Remaining boundary

Nondefault selector values can co-enable all four geometry sites subject to their independent role, view, mode, and active-flag predicates. The earlier four-rectangle synthetic witness remains a valid demonstration that four pairwise-disjoint arbitrary rectangles can exceed the arrays, but it does not establish that the four slot-derived rectangles can simultaneously realize those coordinates through the inset transforms and placement conflict checks. A transitional stale geometry list may also coexist with the single conditional rectangle, for a bounded five-producer list; refresh-before-role-change ordering is unresolved. No gameplay or runtime test was performed.
